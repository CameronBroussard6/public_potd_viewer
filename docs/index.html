Record: 6-3 Last 5: ✅✅❌✅✅ NFL/8:25pm EST/KC@DEN Pick: Kenneth Walker 15+ Receiving Yards Write up:** **Kenneth Walker 15+ receiving yards is one of my favorite ways to attack this matchup because Denver’s biggest strength can actually create the exact type of touches we need. The Broncos led the NFL with 68 sacks last season and blitzed at a high rate, and Kansas City is opening the year without starting left tackle Josh Simmons. Patrick Mahomes is also making his first regular-season start back from the torn ACL, so I expect Andy Reid and Eric Bieniemy to have plenty of answers built in that get the ball out quickly instead of asking Mahomes to hold it against Nik Bonitto, Zach Allen and that front. That is where Walker fits perfectly. Kansas City has him listed as the clear RB1, and the Chiefs spent training camp repeatedly working him into the screen game. In two separate camp practices, the team’s own reporters highlighted Walker taking Mahomes screens for long touchdowns, with the offensive line specifically talking about how dangerous that package can be. This isn’t just projection based on his name either. Walker caught 31 of 36 targets for 282 yards last season, and his most recent football was even better: 29, 49 and 26 receiving yards in Seattle’s three playoff games. He cleared this 15-yard threshold in all three, and 102 of those 104 postseason receiving yards came after the catch. The efficiency is what really sells me on such a small number. Since 2023, Walker has averaged 24.4 receiving yards per game despite running routes on only about 35% of his team’s passing plays, and among qualifying backs he ranks near the top in both targets per route and yards per route. Kansas City didn’t bring in this version of Walker just to hand him the ball between the tackles. They’ve already shown us they want his explosiveness in space, and Denver’s pass rush gives them another reason to lean into that part of the playbook. I can see this cashing on two simple completions, and one well-blocked screen could nearly do it by itself. Against Denver’s pressure package, those touches make even more sense. **Projection: Kenneth Walker III — 3 receptions, 28 receiving yards.**
