Bryce Elder gets the start for Atlanta. He’s 8-7 with a 3.95 ERA and 1.24 WHIP over 143.2 innings. His 2.89 ERA this season looks solid, but that’s only over 9.1 MLB innings, so I’m not putting too much weight into such a small sample. His career MLB ERA is 6.60 and he’s only striking out around 5.9 hitters per nine innings, so I still think there are some pretty big question marks with him. Another thing I like here is the current state of San Francisco’s lineup. They’re missing several important bats, and right now they only have two healthy players with more than 10 home runs this season. That makes me feel a lot better about Elder’s chances of getting through this lineup without needing to be dominant. Atlanta also has the better pitching staff overall. The Braves have a 3.56 team ERA compared with 4.35 for San Francisco, and Atlanta’s bullpen has been much more reliable this season. I also wanted to make sure the price was still reasonable instead of just taking Atlanta because they’re clearly the better team. DRatings has the Braves at a 61.4% chance to win, while another handicap has Atlanta closer to a -184 fair price. At -145, the implied probability is about 59.2%, so there’s still at least some value left at the current number. The biggest concern for me is probably Elder allowing a decent amount of contact, along with the fact that Molina has looked good in his very limited innings this year. That’s why I’m staying away from the -1.5 and just taking Atlanta to win the game. I don’t want a 4-3 Braves win turning into a losing bet. Overall, I like the combination of Atlanta’s home record, the better pitching staff and bullpen, San Francisco’s weaker lineup and the fact that the current price still looks playable. **POTD: Atlanta Braves ML (-145) — 2u** I’d be comfortable playing this around the current number and probably up to about **-160**.
